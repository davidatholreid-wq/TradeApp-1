## 🎨 Visual style guidelines

- **Colour palette**: `#0A0A0A` (surface) · `#FFFFFF` (mark) · one accent per feature (pink for evals, teal for cover, indigo for stock, orange for rewards)
- **Typography (b-roll copy overlays)**: bold sans-serif, all caps, ~5% tracking — matches the wordmark's tone
- **Motion**: slow parallax scroll on mockups (avoid frantic zips), 24 fps for the cinematic parts, 30 fps for UI reveals
- **Music**: cinematic-electronic, moody minor key, gentle build — think Apple product ads
- **VO delivery**: warm, confident, human — never breathless
